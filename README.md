# Zomato_data_analysis_project

## Project Overview

This project aims to analyze Zomato's restaurant data to provide actionable insights into customer behavior and restaurant performance. The goal is to explore various factors such as restaurant ratings, types, spending patterns, and the impact of order modes (online/offline) on customer satisfaction.

By using **Python**, **Pandas**, and **Matplotlib/Seaborn** for data manipulation and visualization, I was able to answer key business questions that would help Zomato improve customer engagement and tailor their promotional strategies.

---

## Key Insights

### 1. **Restaurant Types Preferred by Customers**
- I analyzed the frequency of customer orders by restaurant type.
- **Insight**: Fast food and casual dining restaurants dominate the most ordered categories. This suggests a higher demand for quick, affordable dining options.

### 2. **Votes Distribution Across Restaurant Types**
- A bar plot was created to understand how customers vote for different restaurant types.
- **Insight**: Casual dining and quick bites received the highest number of votes, indicating customer engagement is higher in these segments.

### 3. **Rating Trends Across Restaurants**
- I visualized the ratings distribution to determine which ratings are most frequent.
- **Insight**: A significant majority of restaurants fall within the 3.5 to 4.5 rating range, indicating that customers generally have a positive experience.

### 4. **Average Spending Per Order by Couples**
- Focusing on couples' spending behavior, I calculated the average spending for two people.
- **Insight**: Couples typically spend between ₹500 to ₹1200 per order, which could be useful for designing couple-specific promotions.

### 5. **Online vs Offline Orders: Which Mode Receives the Highest Rating?**
- I compared ratings for online and offline orders to see how they correlate with customer satisfaction.
- **Insight**: Online orders tend to receive higher ratings, likely due to the convenience and user-friendly experience of ordering via the app.

### 6. **Offline Orders by Restaurant Type**
- I identified which restaurant types are favored for offline orders.
- **Insight**: Restaurants such as cafes and casual dining are more frequently visited offline. This can inform Zomato's strategy for offline promotions and targeted offers.

---

## Approach

The analysis was conducted in the following steps:

1. **Data Cleaning**:
   - The dataset was cleaned by handling missing values, converting the `rate` column to a numeric format, and addressing any inconsistencies in the data.

2. **Data Exploration**:
   - Using **Pandas**, I explored the dataset to understand its structure, types, and trends. I performed basic descriptive statistics and visualized the data to answer business questions.

3. **Visualization**:
   - **Matplotlib** and **Seaborn** were used to create various visualizations, including count plots, histograms, and box plots, to better understand the distribution of ratings, votes, and spending behavior.
   - A focus was placed on comparing online and offline orders, as well as analyzing restaurant types to provide actionable insights.

4. **Conclusion**:
   - I concluded that Zomato could leverage customer behavior insights to personalize their offerings. For instance, offline promotions could be targeted toward cafes and casual dining spots, while online order campaigns could focus on improving customer experience to retain high ratings.
